# Donation QR images

Place your payment QR images here. Referenced from `src/data/donate.ts`:

- `banking.webp`  → Banking QR
- `momo.webp`     → MoMo QR
- `zalopay.webp`  → ZaloPay QR
- `alipay.webp`   → Alipay QR
- `wechatpay.webp` → WeChat Pay QR

If a file is missing, the app shows a clean "QR not configured" placeholder
instead of a broken image — so you can ship without all of them.

## Privacy / security
- These are treated as **static local assets** only.
- Served directly from your GitHub Pages deployment.
- Never uploaded to, or processed by, any third-party service.

Recommended: square PNG, at least 512×512px, generated by your own bank/wallet app.
